Free JPG Compressor Online

FileShrink is a free online JPG compressor that helps you reduce JPG and JPEG file sizes while keeping your images looking good. Choose your preferred image quality or enter a target size such as 100 KB or 200 KB.

You can compress one image or multiple JPG images at once. FileShrink processes your images directly in your browser, so your files do not need to be uploaded to a server for compression.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is FileShrink free to use?

Yes. FileShrink is free to use for compressing JPG and JPEG images. There is no account or login required.

Are my images uploaded to a server?

No. FileShrink processes your images directly in your browser. Your images do not need to be uploaded to a FileShrink server for compression.

Can I compress a JPG to a specific size?

Yes. You can enter a target size such as 100 KB or 200 KB, and FileShrink will automatically adjust the compression quality to get as close to that target as possible while staying within the target size.

Can I compress multiple JPG images?

Yes. You can select multiple JPG images and compress them in one batch. You can then download the images individually or download them together as a ZIP file.

Can I use FileShrink on my phone?

Yes. FileShrink works in modern web browsers on phones, tablets, and computers.

What happens if my image is already smaller than the target size?

FileShrink keeps the original image unchanged when it is already smaller than the target size.

🔒 Your image stays on your device. It is not uploaded to a server.
