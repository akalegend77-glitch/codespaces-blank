FileShrink

Privacy Policy

FileShrink is designed to compress JPG images directly in your browser.

Your Images

Your selected images are processed locally on your device. FileShrink does not upload your images to a FileShrink server or store your uploaded images.

No Account Required

FileShrink does not require you to create an account to use the image compression tool.

Downloads

Compressed images are generated in your browser and downloaded directly to your device when you choose to download them.

Third-Party Services

FileShrink currently does not intentionally send your uploaded images to third-party image-processing services.

Changes

This privacy policy may be updated as FileShrink gains new features or services.
